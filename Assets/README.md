We are a small group of students called Pong stuidos. This game, called Aeldrinfall, is our first game. Aeldrinfall is a 2d story-heavy open world rpg with top-down view.

Our game takes place in a world called Aeldrin. This world runs on 7 rules of nature, which were firstly written by 7 different forces. Later, those forces became the 7 gods, known as Aer, Aloterra, Rodo, Illed, Lived, Kreanos and Umebr.

The main character wakes up in a hospital, laying next to a cleric with a strange glow in his chest.
